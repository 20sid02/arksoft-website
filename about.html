About ArkSoft Industries

ArkSoft Industries is a technology company that develops enterprise systems and software products for organisations that require control, accountability and reliability in their operations.

Overview

Who we are

ArkSoft Industries delivers solutions in security and access control, asset and inventory management, and operations and administration. Our systems are developed in-house and configured to the requirements of each organisation.

We manage the complete project lifecycle, from requirement analysis and solution design through development, deployment, training and post-deployment support.

Alongside our enterprise solutions, ArkSoft Industries publishes productivity applications for iOS, macOS and the web.
