Application development Controller firmware Hardware integration Site commissioning

ArkSoft started as software work and grew into hardware because the projects demanded it. It is hard to promise that a rack slot opened for the right person when someone else owns the board that opened it. So we took on that half too.

What we don't do is pretend the boundary is somewhere else. We don't weld, run conduit or sign off electrical work. Where a job needs one of those, we find the right person and coordinate them. The work stays theirs, and we say so at the start.

Everything ships to a real environment: no internet, one machine, staff trained once, and someone who will want to know what happened eleven months ago. That shapes most of the decisions on the rest of this site.

What to send

A first message can be short. These four usually cover it:

  • The operation. What happens today, and who does it.
  • The proof. What has to be recorded, and who reads it later.
  • The hardware. Any readers, locks, printers or machines already there.
  • The site. Where, how many, and whether there is a network at all.
