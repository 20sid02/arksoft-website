Systems

What we've built.

Working systems, not proposals. Most started as one organisation's problem and were built out into something that configures for the next.

01 Hardware + software

CLASP

Controlled Locking, Armoury Security Platform

Weapon issue and return. A soldier puts a finger on the scanner at the kiosk, CLASP works out what they are entitled to, and releases only those rack slots. Sensors confirm what was actually taken. Returns run the same way in reverse, and the record can't be altered after the fact. The application, the lock controllers, the firmware on them and the fingerprint bridge are all ours.

Biometric issue Solenoid locking Entitlement rules Offline
02 Hardware + software

Access Control System

QR card-based entry and exit

Registration, verification, QR-coded PVC card issue and printing, then live scanning at as many gates as the site has. Guards scan from an ordinary phone camera, so a new gate costs a browser tab rather than a terminal. Validity and expiry rules sit behind it, and every scan lands in the log.

Card printing Multi-gate Entry-exit log Live dashboard
03 Hardware + software

Kavach AppAudit

Device compliance kiosk

A self-service kiosk at the entry point. A visitor plugs in an Android phone, taps Allow, and gets an immediate verdict against the banned-applications list with a printed report. Apps are matched on their package identifier rather than their display name, so a renamed app is still caught. It reads the model, serial and installed-app list, and nothing else.

Kiosk build Read-only Printed report Editable ban list
04 Software

Issue & Demand Management

Entitlement-based distribution

Headcounts and entitlement scales become precise demands. Stock is received into store, allocated across competing claims, and issued against a printed authorization stating exactly what may be collected. Every figure can be traced back to the scale that produced it. Configures for rations, equipment, consumables or anything else a central store hands out.

Explainable calculation Receipt vouchers Kiosk with scanner

07 Software

FixDesk

Maintenance and complaint ticketing

A complaint raised from a QR code on the wall reaches the right trade automatically, runs against an SLA clock set by its priority, and closes only once an admin has verified the work. For hostels, hotels, offices, societies, schools, hospitals and factories. Anywhere the gap between reported and actually fixed needs a number on it.

SLA tracking QR locations Photo evidence Reports
08 Software

DASE

Digital Assessment & Selection Engine

Replaces an assessment pipeline that ran on paper and spreadsheets and took a day to produce a merit list. Invigilators enter scores on tablets bound to their own batch, each record locks on submit, and the head of exam watches progress live. The ranked list is generated and approved in the same session. Runs on a closed local network.
