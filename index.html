What we do

Both halves, or it doesn't work.

A locking system is only as good as the moment the software asks a physical device to do something and has to know it happened. We build both sides of that moment.

Software

Applications · kiosks · dashboards

Everything runs on one machine in the building. No cloud account, no subscription, nothing to renew.

  • Roles and audit trails. Submitted records lock, and overrides are logged.
  • Kiosk, tablet and phone screens. One action each, nothing to install.
  • Printed output. Numbered reports and authorizations, produced on the machine.
  • Live dashboards. Gate activity, stock, open tickets, batch progress.
  • Phone and desktop apps for the products we publish ourselves.

Hardware

Firmware · controllers · readers

Controller boards, bench-tested against real relays and sensors before anything is screwed to a rack.

  • Solenoid locking. Relay driven, with the unlock window enforced on the board itself.
  • Slot sensing. Debounced, so a knocked rack does not read as a removal.
  • Biometrics. Fingerprint readers wired straight into the application.
  • Scanners and cards. QR and RFID readers, and card issue and printing.
  • Device checks. Read-only interrogation of Android handsets over USB.
